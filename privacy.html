Privacy Policy

Pageflow Gradients · Effective October 7, 2026

This policy explains how the Pageflow Gradients app for Canva ("the app") handles information. The app is made by Jakob Cansler ("I", "me").

In short

The app does not collect, store or share personal data. It has no server of its own. Everything it remembers stays in your browser on your device.

What the app stores on your device

To bring back your work the next time you open it, the app saves these in your browser's local storage:

  • your current gradient (colors, dot positions, style and slider settings),
  • palettes you choose to save, and their names,
  • interface preferences, such as whether the Templates section is open.

This information is never sent to me or to anyone else. You can delete it by clearing your browser's site data.

What the app accesses in Canva

The app uses permissions that Canva grants to apps, only to do what you ask:

  • Brand kit (read): so Canva's color picker can show your brand colors.
  • Design content (read and write): to read your pages' sizes and order, and to set gradient backgrounds when you ask it to.
  • Assets (write): to upload the gradient image you add to your design. It is stored by Canva, like any other image in your design.

The app does not send this information anywhere outside Canva. Your use of Canva itself is covered by Canva's privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

The app uses no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party cookies.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, for example by adding an option to sync palettes between devices, I will update this policy and its effective date before that change is released.
