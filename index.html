Pageflow Gradients

A gradient app for Canva

Pageflow Gradients makes mesh, linear and radial gradients inside the Canva editor. It can lay one continuous gradient across several pages, so carousels and slide decks join up without a seam. It also remembers your last gradient and your saved palettes.

Getting started

  1. Open a design in Canva and find Pageflow Gradients under Apps.
  2. Pick colors with Canva's color picker, which includes your brand and document colors. Drag the dots on the preview to move them.
  3. Choose a style (Mesh, Linear or Radial), or start from a template.
  4. Click Add to design to insert it as an image, or use it as a page background on this page, on every page, or flowing across pages.

Common questions

My saved palettes are missing on another computer. Palettes are stored in the browser on each device and aren't synced between devices or browsers. Clearing your browser's site data also removes them.

Flow across pages skipped a page. Locked pages are left unchanged. Unlock the page and apply the gradient again.

The From page and To page options are missing. They appear when your design has three or more pages.
